\section{Charging}
\label{sec:charging}

The device is charged through its terminals from a DC supply with \qtyrange{15}{24}{\volt}.
\todo[inline]{Which jacks and polarity? Required supply current? Is a specific charger delivered?}

\begin{steps}
    \item Switch the device on (main menu).
    \item Connect the supply (\qtyrange{15}{24}{\volt}, at least \tbc{\qty{2}{\ampere}}) to \tbc{the output jacks}.
    \item The device starts charging automatically and shows the \disp{Charge} screen.
    \item Charging ends automatically (\disp{Charging finished}).
\end{steps}

The charge screen shows the battery voltage (large), the cell voltages (\disp{Cells}),
the charge current, the state of charge, the charging time and the phase
(\disp{PRECHARGE}, \disp{CLOSE}, \disp{CC}, \disp{CV}).
\btn{ESC} stops charging. Charging does not restart before the supply has been disconnected
\tbc{and reconnected}.

\begin{itemize}
    \item Charge current: \qty{1}{\ampere}, then constant voltage up to \qty{14.0}{\volt} (\qty{3.5}{\volt} per cell).
    \item Charging time: max. \qty{5}{\hour} (\disp{ERROR: Charge timeout}).
    \item Charging only ends once the cells are balanced (difference \qty{\le50}{\milli\volt}).
          Otherwise \disp{Finished, cells unbalanced} is shown; cells can be balanced under
          \disp{Settings > BMS Diagnostics}.
    \item Deeply discharged battery: the device charges automatically with a reduced current
          (\qty{100}{\milli\ampere}) until the battery has recovered.
\end{itemize}

\begin{caution}
    Do not connect a supply above \qty{24}{\volt}. Do not charge outside \tbc{\qtyrange{0}{45}{\celsius}}.
\end{caution}
