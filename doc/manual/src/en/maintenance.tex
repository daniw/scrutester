\section{Maintenance}
\label{sec:maintenance}

\subsection{Cleaning}
Switch the device off and remove all leads. Clean the enclosure with a dry or slightly damp cloth.
Do not use solvents.

\subsection{Storage}
Store the device charged, dry and at \tbc{\qtyrange{0}{50}{\celsius}}. Recharge it every \tbc{3 months}.
\todo[inline]{Self-discharge / standby current in the off state}

\subsection{Calibration interval}
Check the calibration every \tbc{12 months} and before important inspections (\autoref{sec:calibration}).

\subsection{Battery replacement}
The battery can be replaced with basic tools (screwdriver, pliers); no soldering is required.
