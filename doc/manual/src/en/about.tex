\section{About this manual}
\label{sec:about}

This manual describes the operation of the \ST{} for users (\autoref{sec:overview} to \autoref{sec:specs})
and the service tasks calibration and firmware update (\autoref{sec:service}).
It applies to the hardware and firmware versions listed on the title page.
The firmware version of a device is shown under \disp{Settings > About}.
