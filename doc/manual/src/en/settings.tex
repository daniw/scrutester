\section{Settings}
\label{sec:settings}

Select \disp{Settings} in the main menu and press \btn{OK}. Select an entry with \enc{}
and open it with \btn{OK}. \btn{ESC} returns.

\begin{tabular}{lp{10.5cm}}
    \rowcolor{gray} \textbf{Entry} & \textbf{Content} \\
    \disp{BMS Diagnostics}    & Safety alerts/status of the \ac{BMS}, cell voltages, battery current,
                                charge counter. \btn{OK} switches cell balancing on/off. \\
    \disp{Display Brightness} & Ambient light and resulting display brightness (automatic). \\
    \disp{Calibration}        & Calibration of the measurement channels, see \autoref{sec:calibration}. \\
    \disp{ADC Readings 1/2}   & Raw and converted measurement values (service). \\
    \disp{About}              & Firmware version, build date, serial number, hardware revision. \\
\end{tabular}
